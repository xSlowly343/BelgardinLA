# Arena references

## Arena 1: 거룩한 안식의 묘원 (Holy Rest Cemetery), gate I, Belgardin

Files in `arena1/`:

- `01-overview-official.jpg`: official overview from the GM note, 900×500. It is the best layout reference.
- `02-sky-cutscene.jpg`: the 75-bar cutscene. Use it for the sky.
- `03-boss-in-arena-wide.jpg`: Belgardin over the arena, with the crypt on the right.
- `04-floor-coffins.jpg`: the floor with standing coffins and violet mist.
- `05` to `07`: gameplay frames of the floor (720p).

What to build:

- **Platform.** A round platform about 21 m across, the size of the arena circle. It is made of dark grey flagstones laid in radial rows, with a raised rim step around the edge. Two thin pale rings are inlaid in it, an outer one and an inner one, and the centre holds a cracked sigil disc.
- **Surroundings.** The platform sits in a sunken, misty graveyard. The ground there is dark earth, crooked headstones and iron fences. Small warm lights burn among the graves: candles, braziers and ember bowls.
- **Mid ground.**
  - A gothic crypt with a clock face and a spire, standing behind the "north" edge.
  - Hooded statues on plinths.
  - Bare dead trees with long twisted branches.
  - Broken pillars and arches.
- **Far ground.** Cathedral spires, dark pine silhouettes and ruined walls, all fading into fog.
- **Sky.** Low storm clouds. A dark circular dome or vortex hangs overhead with violet light leaking around it, and black blobs and ash drift through the air.
- **Palette.** Near-monochrome: charcoal, slate and cold lilac fog. The only warm accents are small orange flames, plus the violet magic glow.

## Arena 2: 크라그마의 사멸지 (Kragma's Annihilation Ground), gate II, Kragma

Files in `arena2/`:

- `01-platform-cultists.jpg`: platform with white rune rings, black glossy void and red-robed cultists.
- `02-floor-ritual.jpg`: the floor during the 360 ritual, with red ember flecks and cultist statues on the rim.
- `03` to `05`: gameplay frames (720p) of the carved disc floor and the tentacles lying on it.

- `00-sky-official.png`: the official GM-note image of the sky, with a blue-violet nebula and Kragma's ring, 1000×500.

What to build:

- **Platform.** A round stone platform floating in space, 21 m across. Its surface is carved with concentric ridges and radial segments, like a giant engraved disc, and a smaller inner disc sits in the centre. Thin white or lilac rune rings glow on it.
- **Edge.** The edge is a sheer drop, and falling off kills. The field shrinks twice during the fight (240 and about 135 bars, "Terrain Destruction"), so the outer ring should be breakable or separable into chunks.
- **Below and around.** Black glossy void or water under and around the platform.
- **Rim.** Cultists in red, blood-stained robes with tall pointed hoods stand around the rim, frozen mid-ritual. Flesh pillars or ritual objects mark the 3, 6, 9 and 12 o'clock positions (360 ritual).
- **Sky.** A violet-pink nebula with blue wisps, floating debris and red embers.
- **Palette.** Deep violet and indigo with black stone. Accents are the white rune light, the blood-red cultists and the embers.
